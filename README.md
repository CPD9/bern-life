# Bern Life

**Ask in your words. Get the right Bern experts, each with the sentence that proves it.**

A Research & Expertise Navigator for the University of Bern, the ARTORG Center and Diabetes Center
Berne, built on Apertus v1.5 and fully self-hostable. Every person, role, project and facility it
shows links to the public page that says so. When no page supports an answer, it says so.

Work in progress for HackApertus Liebefeld (feature freeze 18 Oct 2026, 23:00). The start page,
animated explainers and one-command demo land during the day; until then see
[`docs/case.md`](docs/case.md) for the case and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) to take part.

Apache-2.0 (code). Source pages belong to their institutions; see [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) and
[`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md).
